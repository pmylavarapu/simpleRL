## Normal antegrade Doppler velocities

| Site | Normal range (m/s) |
|---|---|
| Ascending aorta | 1.0 – 1.7 |
| LVOT | 0.7 – 1.1 |
| Mitral E (early) | 0.6 – 1.3 |
| Mitral A (late) | 0.2 – 0.7 |
| Pulmonary artery | 0.5 – 1.3 |
| RV inflow E | 0.3 – 0.7 |
| SVC / hepatic vein (systolic) | 0.32 – 0.69 |
| Pulmonary vein (systolic) | 0.56 ± 0.13 |
| Pulmonary vein (diastolic) | 0.44 ± 0.16 |
| Pulmonary vein (atrial reversal) | 0.32 ± 0.07 |

## Sequence for a routine exam

- PW at LVOT to derive stroke volume (with CSA_LVOT).
- CW across AV, MV, TV, PV for peak velocities and gradients.
- PW at mitral inflow (leaflet tips) for E, A, DT.
- Tissue Doppler at septal and lateral mitral annulus for e′ (diastolic function).
- PW of pulmonary vein (S, D, atrial reversal).
- PW of hepatic vein and IVC (RA pressure estimation).
- Color Doppler for regurgitant/shunt lesions in every view.

## Age-related changes in transmitral inflow

- Young adults: E > A, E/A > 1.
- Middle age: E/A ≈ 1.
- Older adults: E/A < 1 (impaired relaxation is the norm).
- With age: E velocity ↓, deceleration time ↑, A velocity ↑.

## IAC (Intersocietal Accreditation Commission)

- The primary accrediting body for echocardiography laboratories in the United States.
- Accreditation covers adult TTE, adult TEE, adult stress echo, and adult congenital echo (as separate testing areas).

## Requirements — high level

- **Personnel**: board-certified/board-eligible cardiologists (or equivalent) and credentialed sonographers (ARDMS-registered in adult echo, typically).
- **Volume**: minimum annual case volumes documented.
- **Quality assurance**: periodic peer review of studies (recommended ≥ 10 % of studies).
- **Report turnaround** and preliminary vs. final read separation.
- **Continuing medical education**: ongoing hours in echocardiography.
- **Written protocols** for each study type.

## Why accreditation matters

- Payer requirement (many insurers require IAC-accredited labs for reimbursement).
- Quality assurance signal for referrers.
- Structured process for continuous improvement.
